# St. Lucia Airport Transfer Guide

A practical guide for travelers arriving in St. Lucia, maintained by [TripCarib](https://tripcarib.com), a local airport transfer, car rental and excursion service.

## St. Lucia has two airports

| Airport | Code | Location | Typical flights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hewanorra International | UVF | Vieux Fort, south of the island | Most international flights from the US, UK and Canada |
| George F. L. Charles | SLU | Castries, north of the island | Mainly regional Caribbean flights |

Check which airport is on your ticket before you book a transfer. The two airports are at opposite ends of the island, and many travelers only find out on arrival.

## Before you land

- Have your flight number ready so your driver can track delays
- Know your hotel or villa name and the area it is in
- Count passengers and bags so the right vehicle is sent
- Book your transfer in advance during peak season

## Main areas travelers head to

- **Rodney Bay and Gros Islet** in the north, home to many resorts, restaurants and the marina
- **Castries** in the north, the capital and cruise port
- **Marigot Bay** on the west coast
- **Soufriere** on the southwest coast, near the Pitons
- **Vieux Fort** in the south, beside Hewanorra airport
